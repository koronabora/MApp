# Bundled decoder

MApp 0.2.0 uses VideoLAN LibVLC Android 3.7.7 (shared native libraries),
distributed via Maven Central as `org.videolan.android:libvlc-all:3.7.7`.
Copyright VideoLAN and contributors. LibVLC is licensed under LGPL 2.1 or later.
Source and build instructions: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/vlc-android
License: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html

The APK includes arm64-v8a for phones and x86_64 for emulator verification.
